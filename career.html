What it takes to survive

Becoming a licensed veterinarian requires a challenging academic process that begins with a rigorous four year undergraduate degree. After the first four years of college, you need to complete a 2 to 4 year program (vet school) to finally earn you Doctor of Veterinary Medicine (DVM). The first three years of veterinary schoold demand intense dedication to master complex subjects like anatomy and pathololgy. Students them may spend their final year doing hands on clinical rotations, treating real patients under supervsion of a certified doctor.Upon graduating, you must pass the (NAVLE) exame to legally practice medicine. Many new veterinarians choose to pursue optional, internships that are able to provie you with years of practice to finnally become a doctor.Ultimately, the career demands a life long commitment to helping pets in our community thrive.
